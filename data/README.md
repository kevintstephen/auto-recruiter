# Data directory (gitignored)

Put two files here before the first run:

1. **connections.csv** — your LinkedIn connections export.
   LinkedIn → Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → "Get a copy of your data" → Connections.
   Columns: First Name, Last Name, Company, Position, Connected On.
   Re-export every month or two to keep warm-path matching fresh.

2. **resume.md** — your positioning doc. Paste your resume or a richer narrative
   version. The drafting stage pulls specifics from here.
